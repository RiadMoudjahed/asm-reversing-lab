# Code of Conduct

This project is a learning space. Everyone who takes part, as a learner, contributor or
maintainer, agrees to the following.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Learners arrive with very different backgrounds.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Accept corrections graciously and credit other people's contributions.
- Write in clear, professional language in all project spaces.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or insults.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Posting complete exercise solutions in public issues or discussions.
- Requests for help with unauthorized access to systems or software you do not have the
  right to analyze, which is also outside this project's scope (see `ETHICS.md`).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block participants who violate
this code. Report concerns to the maintainers through the contact method listed in the
repository's profile. Reports are handled privately.
